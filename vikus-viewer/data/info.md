# Cybersyn Boxes (Chile, 1971-1973) 

This visualisation prototype is based on the "Cybersyn" project archives, part of the Stafford Beer Archive held at Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU) in the United Kingdom. The archive comprises several sections, one of which is dedicated to the Cybersyn Project developed in Chile. This section consists of 16 boxes, 10 of which (numbered 55 through 64) are utilised in this project. 

![tags](img/infobar_tags.svg)

These boxes contain a variety of materials, including manuscripts, diagrams, official documents, and photographs. This is an initial prototype based on CSV database. 

![time](img/infobar_time.svg)

This visualisation project makes a modest contribution to recognising the work of thinker and creator Stafford Beer, inspired by the spirit that united people in Chile during the turbulent early 1970s—a spirit driven by the vision of building a better world. 
Thanks to: John Vines, Mellisa Terras, Uta Heinrich, Tomas Vascicin, Constantin Malik, LJMU, ALicia Vega & María Jose Ríos.
