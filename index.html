Ricardo Vega Mora

Diseño • Arte de Datos / Design • Data Art / Santiago, Chile

Ricardo is a designer, artist, programmer and cultural manager. He obtained an MFA in Design and Technology from Parsons, The New School for Design, and has studied Information Visualisation and Data Science. He is a professor at the School of Design and has also taught at the School of Engineering, both at the Catholic University of Chile. He directs the Diploma in Data Visualisation at Diseño UC. He is interested in transdisciplinary links, carrying out studies in Philosophy and Science. He develops projects at the intersection of visualisation, graphics, programming, art, and design, exploring the aesthetic and subjective representation of data. He is the founder of C80.cl, a project that has, since 2015, used visualisations to support the contextualised understanding of the Chilean Constitution of 1980. As an artist, he has participated in exhibitions, conferences and presentations in Chile, Argentina, Brazil, Spain, and Estonia, among other places.

INTERESTS: Social and cultural implications of technology; Complex Systems; Creative Programming; Information Design; Data Visualisation; Transdiscipline; Convergence of Arts and Sciences; Digital Humanities; Technology, Data and Society.
